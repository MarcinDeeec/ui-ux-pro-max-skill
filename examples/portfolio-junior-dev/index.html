// 02 — about

A junior dev with a senior obsession for the basics.

I switched from QA into frontend in 2023 after one too many tickets about “the button you can’t reach with Tab.” That experience shaped how I work today: I treat semantics, contrast and keyboard support as features, not afterthoughts.

Most days I’m in React + TypeScript, sometimes Next.js. I care about small bundles, predictable state, and a design system you can trust at 2 am.

Outside the editor: rock climbing, mechanical keyboards, and trying to brew espresso that doesn’t taste like burnt rubber.
